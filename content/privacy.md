You can read, search and download every book on Foggy Press without an account. An account is optional: it keeps the books you save, your reading lists, your own tags and your reading history, so they follow you to any device.

## What we keep

If you make an account, we keep your email address, the books you save, your lists and tags with their notes, and your reading history: which books you opened, where you stopped, and whether you downloaded the PDF or EPUB. You can turn history off or clear it at any time on the My books page.

If you use Ask Foggy Press while signed in, we keep your questions and the answers as past chats in your account, and a count of how many questions you ask each day (for the daily limit). You can also ask without an account: then we keep the chat for 30 days with no name or email attached, so that it can continue, and a count of questions from a scrambled form of your internet address (for the daily limit). Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're a person before a question is answered. If you request a book, we keep the request (title, author, your link and note) and what happened to it, and email you when it's done.

We don't keep your name, a password or any payment details. There are no ads and no tracking cookies. We never sell or share what you save, and we only email you to sign you in.

Without an account, the books you save and the ones you read recently are kept in this browser only, in a small cookie.

## Who can see it

Only you, when you are signed in. The site's owner can see the list of account email addresses in the account service, and totals such as how many people saved a book, but never one person's books. If you give an answer from Ask Foggy Press a thumbs up or down, the site's owner can read that question and answer, and any note you add, without your email or account, to make Ask better. Our visitor counts (GoatCounter) are anonymous totals and are not linked to accounts.

Ask Foggy Press answers with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic: your question and the earlier messages in that chat are sent to Anthropic to write the answer. Anthropic does not use them to train its models. Answers are written by AI and can be wrong; the quotes link to the book so you can check them.

## Where it is kept

Accounts are stored with Supabase in the United States. Its logs keep the internet address of sign-in requests for a short time, for security. Sign-in emails are sent by Resend.

## Your choices

On the My books page you can download everything kept about you as a file, or delete your account. On the Ask page you can delete past chats one by one or all at once. Deleting removes the account and everything in it at once; backup copies are gone within 7 days. Accounts are for readers 13 and over.
