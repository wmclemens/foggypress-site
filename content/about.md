Foggy Press sets public-domain books in one plain house style and publishes them as free PDFs, ready to print at home on US letter paper.

## Rights and sources

The books here are public-domain texts in the United States, where Foggy Press is based: works whose copyright has expired or was never renewed. The one exception is David Wyllie’s English translation of Kafka’s *Metamorphosis*, which Project Gutenberg distributes free with the translator’s permission. If you live outside the United States, copyright terms can differ, so please check your own country’s rules before you copy or reprint a book.

Our texts come from public-domain transcriptions and scans: mostly [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org), and also the [Internet Archive](https://archive.org), [Wikisource](https://wikisource.org), [HathiTrust](https://www.hathitrust.org) and [Google Books](https://books.google.com). We reset each text in our own layout and add nothing but page headings and, for collections, a short contents list. Each book’s record names its original publication date and, where there is one, its translator.

Foggy Press is a free, non-commercial reading edition. There are no ads, no accounts and nothing for sale.

## Corrections and takedown requests

If you believe a book on this site is still under copyright, or that we have made a mistake about its rights, please tell us through the form below. Name the book and explain the problem, and we will look into it promptly. The same form works for typos and other errors.
